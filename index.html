RETER

DETECT · VERIFY · ALERT · RESPOND

An open-source concept exploring AI-assisted road-incident detection and coordinated emergency response using authorized CCTV infrastructure.

Open-source concept Human verification Early stage

The goal

Explore ways to reduce the time between a road incident being noticed and appropriate emergency responders being notified, while keeping human operators in control.

Project status

Early-stage concept and documentation

This site describes the intended workflow. It is not a working accident-detection or emergency-dispatch system.

Proposed workflow

CCTV captureRegistered, authorized camera
AI detectionFlags a possible incident
Two-operator reviewRequired for serious incidents
Emergency alertOnly after required approvals
Response trackingAcknowledgement to closure

Safety by design

  • AI predictions do not dispatch emergency services by themselves.
  • Serious incidents require two different authorized operators to approve.
  • Disagreement or timeout triggers escalation, never automatic approval.
  • Use only authorized camera feeds and emergency-service integrations.

Planned components

  • Camera registry and fixed-location mapping
  • Incident review dashboard
  • Role-based access and audit logs
  • Authorized ambulance and notification integrations

Roadmap

  1. Define requirements with road-safety and emergency-response stakeholders.
  2. Design camera registry, incident records, and operator permissions.
  3. Build the incident review and dual-approval workflow.
  4. Research and evaluate accident-detection models.
  5. Integrate approved response partners and test in a controlled environment.

Responsible use

RETER is not an emergency service. In an actual emergency, contact local emergency services directly.

Any real deployment must comply with applicable privacy and data-protection laws, use authorized CCTV feeds, protect evidence, validate model performance, and verify message delivery and responder status rather than assuming dispatch occurred.
